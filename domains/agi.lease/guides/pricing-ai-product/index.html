How to Price an AI Product Without Torching Your Margins

Usage-based costs make AI pricing uniquely dangerous. Get it right early.

Share:X / TwitterLinkedIn

Inference costs money per call. Price wrong and you scale straight into negative margins. This is the pricing discipline AI founders skip at their peril.

Know your cost per action

Before you set a price, know what a typical user's monthly usage costs you in tokens and compute. Many AI startups discover their power users are wildly unprofitable at a flat subscription price.

Value-based, with usage guardrails

Anchor price to the value delivered — the hours saved or revenue generated — not to your costs. But add usage caps or metered overage so a heavy user can't blow up your margins.

Charge sooner than feels comfortable

Free tiers train users to expect free. Introduce paid plans early; a small paying base teaches you more about value than a large free one ever will.

Key takeaways

  • Calculate cost-per-user before naming a price.
  • Anchor to value, cap usage to protect margins.
  • Start charging earlier than feels comfortable.
