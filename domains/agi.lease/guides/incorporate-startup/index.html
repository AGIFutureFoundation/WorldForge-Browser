Incorporating Your Startup: The 1-Day Setup

Delaware C-Corp, cap table, and banking — the boring stuff that kills deals if wrong.

Share:X / TwitterLinkedIn

Investors expect a clean Delaware C-Corp. Get the legal foundation right in a day so it never becomes a diligence problem later.

Delaware C-Corp, almost always

If you plan to raise from US VCs, incorporate as a Delaware C-Corp. LLCs and foreign entities create friction that spooks investors and complicates SAFEs.

The one-day stack

Modern tools like Stripe Atlas, Clerky, or Firstbase handle incorporation, EIN, and templates in under a day for a few hundred dollars. Pair with a startup-friendly bank (Mercury, Brex) and you're operational immediately.

File your 83(b) — do not forget

Founders on vesting stock must file an 83(b) election within 30 days of the grant. Miss it and you can owe taxes as your equity vests. This single form has cost founders millions.

Key takeaways

  • Delaware C-Corp if you'll raise from US VCs.
  • Use Atlas/Clerky/Firstbase to set up in a day.
  • File your 83(b) within 30 days — no exceptions.
