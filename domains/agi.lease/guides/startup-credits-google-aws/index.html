How to Get $350K+ in Startup Credits from Google, AWS & Azure

The full stack of cloud and AI credits early-stage founders leave on the table.

Share:X / TwitterLinkedIn

Compute is the single largest line item for most AI startups in year one. The good news: the three major clouds plus a dozen AI vendors will hand early-stage teams six figures of credits — if you know which door to knock on. This is the current map.

Google for Startups Cloud Program

Two tiers. The Start tier gives up to $2,000 in credits with no strings. The Scale tier — for AI-first and funded startups — offers up to $350,000 in Google Cloud credits over two years, plus dedicated technical support and Vertex AI access.

The fastest path in is through a partner accelerator or VC. Being in a recognized program moves you to the Scale tier without a funding requirement. (Yes, the AGI Accelerator counts.)

AWS Activate

AWS Activate Founders gives up to $1,000 self-serve. Activate Portfolio — unlocked through an accelerator, incubator, or VC — gives up to $100,000 in credits valid for two years, plus $350 in AWS Support and technical office hours.

Stack Activate with the Bedrock and SageMaker free tiers to run inference and fine-tuning experiments before you spend a dollar.

Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub

No funding or accelerator required to start. You get up to $150,000 in Azure credits across stages, plus free access to Azure OpenAI Service, GitHub Enterprise, and LinkedIn Premium.

This is the most self-serve of the three — apply directly and you can be provisioned in days.

The AI vendor layer

Beyond the clouds: OpenAI, Anthropic, and others run startup credit programs, usually gated behind accelerators or partner networks. NVIDIA Inception gives hardware discounts and DGX Cloud credits. HubSpot, Notion, Stripe, and Brex all run startup deals worth thousands more.

Key takeaways

  • Apply to Microsoft Founders Hub today — it needs no accelerator and provisions fast.
  • Use an accelerator or VC to unlock the Scale/Portfolio tiers on Google and AWS.
  • Stack cloud credits with vendor free tiers before you pay for any compute.
