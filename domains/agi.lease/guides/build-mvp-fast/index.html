Building an AI MVP in 2 Weeks

Scope ruthlessly, ship the wedge, learn from real users.

Share:X / TwitterLinkedIn

An MVP proves one hypothesis with real users. Two weeks is plenty if you're honest about scope.

Define the one thing

Write the single hypothesis your MVP tests. Everything that doesn't serve that test gets cut. No auth flows, no settings pages, no polish that doesn't affect the core value.

Buy, don't build

Use frontier model APIs, auth providers, and off-the-shelf UI. Your differentiation is the wedge, not the plumbing. Building infrastructure at MVP stage is procrastination in disguise.

Ship to real users, not friends

Friends lie kindly. Get the MVP in front of people who'd actually pay, and measure behavior, not compliments.

Key takeaways

  • Cut everything that doesn't test your core hypothesis.
  • Buy infrastructure, build only your wedge.
  • Validate with real potential customers, not friends.
