Go-to-Market for AI Startups: Getting Your First 100 Users

Distribution is the moat now that models are commoditized.

Share:X / TwitterLinkedIn

In a world where anyone can wrap a frontier model, distribution wins. Your first 100 users teach you whether you have a product — get them by hand.

Do things that don't scale

Your first users should be onboarded personally. DM them, jump on calls, watch them use the product. The goal isn't growth yet — it's learning why they stay or leave.

Pick one channel and dominate it

Founders spread across five channels and win none. Choose one where your users already congregate — a subreddit, an X community, a Discord, a specific conference — and become a fixture there before adding a second.

Build in public

Sharing your journey, demos, and even failures compounds. It builds an audience that converts to users and, later, to investors. Every guide in this pack is designed to be shared for exactly this reason.

Key takeaways

  • Onboard your first 100 users by hand and learn from every churn.
  • Own one distribution channel before touching a second.
  • Build in public — attention compounds into users and capital.
