Corporate sponsorship

Ways to support

Three tiers, each tied to concrete work. All sponsorship supports open, published, non-proprietary efforts.

Lead partner

Guardian

Named support of the Fellowship's core work — the alignment curriculum, Digital Lodges, and governance. Recognition across the site and at flagship gatherings.

  • ✓Named recognition on the website
  • ✓Speaking slot at a flagship gathering
  • ✓Quarterly briefing with stewards
Program partner

Steward

Support a specific program — a lecture series, a Lodge, or a community event — with recognition tied to that program.

  • ✓Program-level recognition
  • ✓Invitations to sponsored events
  • ✓Annual impact summary
Community supporter

Seeker

Back the Fellowship's open-source work and community engagement at a grassroots level.

  • ✓Listing on the Partners page
  • ✓Community updates
  • ✓Our gratitude

Community engagement

Academic & research
