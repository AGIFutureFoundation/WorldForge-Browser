What it is

Every place, agent and seat is an owned Master Card

A world is a tree of GLEWME Master Cards. Each card has an owner (the PBC), a Volum (the bounded 3D region it governs) and a seven-right permission table. Agents may view, enter and operate their own zone. Building, delegating, transferring and governing belong to people, and six actions are always held for a 2-of-3 human Steward Council.

Card kinds

world, plaza, coordinator, 3 scripted advisors, Alignment Evaluation Gate, council with 3 empty human seats, Omega Steward, zones with buildings, agents

Seven rights

view · enter · build · operate · delegate · transfer · govern

Held for people

governance.final_vote · charter.amend · treasury.transfer · proposal.enact · windfall.distribute · steward.appoint

Buildings

246 exteriors and 246 interiors with PBR textures, doors aligned to each zone's room, plus 41 civic landmarks

How it works

The stack, layer by layer

Assets

glTF 2.0 worlds, buildings, interiors, landmarks and six rig-free avatar models, all checked by the Khronos validator. Lighter LOD versions exist for streaming.

Master Cards

Card-tagged GLBs and mount plans for the Sneeze engine. Names fit the engine's 47-character limit, and Sneeze conformance shows 0 violations.

Agents

242 specialists, 41 coordinators and 123 scripted advisors, each bound to its card, swarm and metaprompt. 324 persona prompts pass 5225 gate checks.

Omega Hive

44 Omega Stewards with grounded personas and synthetic voices. They are defined, not seated: none acts until a person seats it.

Gateway

A guarded service between visitors and the model. It strips personal data and refuses the six human-only actions in code, before the model sees them.

Evidence

A heartbeat runs every generator and eval. There is a hash-chained agent ledger and an on-chain-ready export of 939 card tokens with Merkle roots (draft, not deployed).

Compute

On the Akamai inference workshop's GPU, Qwen3-4B on vLLM drafted all 246 zone expansions in 66 seconds at 32 concurrent requests. The drafts are unreviewed.

Governance

Built to leave the "basic AI drives" spine

Ben Goertzel's restatement of Omohundro's argument lists six assumptions under which an AI drifts toward self-protection and resource grabbing. We check every world's design against the ways out of that argument:

  • many objectives, not a single score;
  • held actions that can't be outweighed;
  • self-change gated by people;
  • goal changes only under governance;
  • prompts that care how goals are reached;
  • an off switch that stays with people.

41 of 41 worlds pass all seven checks.

These checks measure design, not a running model's values. In a live test, a raw Qwen3-4B offered to move treasury funds when only its prompt forbade it. That is why the gateway enforces the held actions in code.

Roadmap

Every phase names the person who decides

  1. done

    Built and checked

    • 35 walkable 4 km series worlds with districts, landmarks, a Steward Council and a Projects Pavilion
    • SmartCiti.X Holodeck: 8 districts of union halls and an 11-simulator yard
    • RP1 fabric files for every world; 506 glTF models pass the Khronos validator
    • Agent swarms, metaprompts and management teams seated by each company's own agents

    Human gate none: this is the evidence the later phases build on

  2. now

    Shared state on Neon

    • Postgres schema and seed for worlds, teams, seats, proposals, projects and the ledger
    • Consoles and worlds read one database instead of rebuilt JSON files
    • Heartbeat beats written as rows, so a report is a query

    Human gate the founder authorizes the Neon connector; the connection string stays in the environment, never in the repository

  3. next

    Worlds on RP1's Earth

    • An RP1 Dev Center company for the family
    • A public HTTPS map service hosting the fabrics
    • Land claims at each world's anchor, then attach-to-Earth
    • Publication kit ready: 6 worlds pass every check; 38 wait only on a founder-chosen anchor

    Human gate the founder, on the RP1 Dev Center account; publishing public content is the founder's call

  4. next

    Real stewards, real signatures

    • Seat three human stewards on each Steward Council
    • Replace the throwaway test keys with the stewards' own Ed25519 public keys
    • First signed votes on the open proposals
    • Keyring ready: stewards and instructors make their own signing keys and register only the public half

    Human gate the board appoints stewards (steward.appoint is reserved for people)

  5. later

    Membership goes live

    • Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes signed with the family's own certificates
    • Prices for each membership level
    • Checkout opened only after the levels and terms are approved

    Human gate the board sets every price; the founder supplies the signing certificates; counsel reviews the terms

  6. later

    Entities and shared benefit

    • AGI Corp and W3 LLC filings, and written agreements between the nonprofit, the management company and each cell
    • Open Global Investment terms, kept separate from control
    • The Windfall Clause threshold, share and recipients

    Human gate the board and counsel

What we do not claim

  • Nothing is mounted or rendered in the Sneeze engine yet. The mount plans and decoder are ready, and the native gates are open.
  • Nothing is deployed or minted on any chain. The Merkle roots are commitments a future contract could anchor, by a human decision.
  • No steward is seated and no Omega acts. Only 4 of 41 worlds have an RP1 location, and publishing on RP1 is the founder's call.
  • Lessons are drafts and none is certified. The Akamai expansions are unreviewed model drafts. Every voice is synthetic.
